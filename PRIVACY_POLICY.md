# Privacy Policy for Monthly Lekka

**Last Updated:** September 4, 2026

Monthly Lekka ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is collected, used, and safeguarded when you use our mobile application **Monthly Lekka** (the "Application").

---

## 1. Information We Collect

Monthly Lekka is designed with a privacy-first approach. The Application stores your financial records locally on your device.

### a. User-Entered Data
- **Expense Records & Accounts:** Expense amounts, categories, dates, descriptions, notes, and budget tables ("Lekkas") created within the application are stored locally on your device using an encrypted/isolated Room database.
- **Photos & Attachments:** If you optionally attach receipt photos to expense entries, these images are stored locally in your app storage.

### b. Device Permissions (Optional)
Depending on the features you use, the Application may request the following permissions:
- **Camera:** Required only if you choose to take photos of receipts or bills directly within the app.
- **Location:** Required only if you choose to attach location metadata to expense entries. Location processing occurs entirely on your device.
- **Storage / Media Access:** Used to attach receipt images and export/import expense summaries locally.

### c. Third-Party Services & Analytics
Monthly Lekka does not sell, rent, trade, or transmit your personal or financial data to third-party advertising networks, data brokers, or external analytics platforms.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information stored on your device strictly to:
- Provide core app functionality: tracking, categorizing, and calculating your monthly expenses.
- Display visual charts, analytics, and budget summaries.
- Enable receipt attachment and localized expense management.

---

## 3. Data Storage and Retention

- **Local Storage:** All expense data, custom categories, and images are saved locally on your device.
- **Control & Deletion:** You have complete control over your data. You can edit or delete entries at any time within the app. Uninstalling the app will clear all local data.

---

## 4. Children's Privacy

Monthly Lekka does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from anyone under the age of 13.

---

## 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any updates will be reflected in this document with an updated "Last Updated" date.
