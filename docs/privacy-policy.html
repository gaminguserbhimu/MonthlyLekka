Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 4, 2026

Monthly Expenses ("we", "our", or "us"), developed by VinApps, is committed to protecting your personal privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Monthly Expenses mobile application.

100% Local & Private by Design

Monthly Expenses operates strictly offline. All your financial records, budget tables ("Lekkas"), receipt attachments, and custom categories remain 100% on your device. We do not store, collect, or transmit your financial data to external servers.

1. Information We Collect

Monthly Expenses is built with an absolute privacy-first philosophy:

  • User-Entered Data: Expense amounts, categories, transaction notes, payday cycles, custom currency settings, and custom Lekka budget tables created within the application are saved locally on your device in an isolated Room database.
  • Photos & Attachments: If you optionally choose to attach receipt images or photos to transactions, these images are stored exclusively in your device's local application storage directory.
  • No User Registration: The application does not require account creation, sign-in credentials, email addresses, or personal phone numbers.

2. Device Permissions

Depending on the features you choose to use, the application may request the following permissions:

  • Camera: Required only if you choose to take photos of paper receipts directly from within the app.
  • Storage / Media Access: Used to attach existing receipt images and export/import expense summaries locally.

3. Data Storage & Security

All data entered into Monthly Expenses remains strictly under your control. Uninstalling the application will permanently remove all local application data from your device unless you have performed a manual local backup.
